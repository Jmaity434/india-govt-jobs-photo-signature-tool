Frequently Asked Questions

What is the exact signature size for SSC forms?

6.0 cm \u00d7 2.0 cm at 300 DPI (708 \u00d7 236 pixels). File size must be strictly between 10 KB and 20 KB in JPG/JPEG format. Signature must be horizontally aligned and clear.

What is the signature requirement for RRB Railway exams?

3.5 cm \u00d7 1.5 cm (140 \u00d7 60 pixels recommended). File size between 30 KB and 70 KB. Must be in running hand / cursive script. Block capitals are not accepted. Black or dark blue ink only.

How to add Name and Date of Photo for UPSC and State PSC exams?

Select UPSC (or Kerala PSC, MPPSC, TNPSC etc.). Enable the Name & Date checkbox, enter the candidate name and photo date, then process. A calibrated white banner is rendered on the bottom 18% of the image with centered typography.

Is my photo uploaded to any server?

No. Every operation (crop, resize, compression) runs entirely inside your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. No image data leaves your device.
